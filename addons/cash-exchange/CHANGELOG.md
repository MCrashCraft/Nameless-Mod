# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — separate cash-exchange addon

- Independent, server-only addon requiring NamelessMC Forge 0.1.1+ and Just Enough Drugz.
- Six physical dollar bill denominations at $1 = 1 Store credit.
- Persistent on/off switch, disabled by default, with operator commands.
- Own verified account only; bounded exact bills from main inventory/hotbar.
- Single-player saved-inventory check, asynchronous credit transport and durable receipts with uncertain outcomes held for manual review.
- No changes to the NamelessMC core JAR, original currency mod, website checkout or client profile.
